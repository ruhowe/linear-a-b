# Davis (2014), *Minoan Stone Vessels with Linear A Inscriptions*

Aegaeum 36 (Peeters, Leuven/Liège). ISBN 9789042930971.

## What it claims

Catalogue of inscribed Minoan stone vessels (libation tables and ladles from Crete and Kythera,
1897 to 2000), with a linguistic analysis. Vowel frequencies given as a 39.3%, i 25.7%, u 18.1%,
e 14.0%, o 2.9% (p. 240-2), with Minoan argued to have five vowels but to descend from, or
originally have had, a three-vowel system (a, i, u), the e- and o-series being later
developments.

## Our assessment

Checked 2026-09-13: could not reach the primary text; no library or personal copy available for
this check. Confirmed ISBN, publisher and page count (xxiv, 419 to 421 pp. depending on the
source counted) via the Bryn Mawr Classical Review of the book (bmcr.brynmawr.edu/2015/2015.11.36),
which does not itself discuss the vowel-frequency figures. The vowel frequencies were confirmed
instead against a later paper that cites them with a page locator, Meißner and Steele's
"Linear A and Linear B: structural and contextual concerns" (`meissner_steele_preprint` in this
catalogue), which gives "Davis 2014, 240-2" for exactly these five percentages. Added that page
locator to the page above. The flag stays **P**: this is a citation in a later paper, not the
primary itself.

The near-absence of o is a central open problem — accidental gap or real phonology.

# Davis (2013), *Syntax in Linear A: the word-order of the libation formula*

Kadmos 52(1): 35–52. The published title puts the subtitle in quotation marks: *Syntax in
Linear A: The Word-Order of the 'Libation Formula'*.

## What it claims

A comparative study of the standard sequences of the Linear A "libation formula" on Minoan
ritual vessels, concluding that the word order is verb-initial, probably VSO, and that the
underlying language is non-Indo-European and non-Semitic.

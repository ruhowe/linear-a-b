# Taylor (1976), review of Packard, *Minoan Linear A*

Daniel J. Taylor, Lawrence University. A short review on JSTOR; the journal title was not recorded when the text was copied.

## What it claims

Packard's aim is twofold: to classify the tablets by apparent subject matter, and "to examine on the basis of non-etymological evidence possible phonetic values for some of the Linear A symbols". The reviewer adds that "in view of the paucity of Linear A material an independent decipherment, such as was possible with Linear B, is unlikely". The rest praises Packard's statistics and computer typesetting.

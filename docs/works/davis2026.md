# Davis (2026), *The Undeciphered Aegean Scripts: Linguistic Investigations into the Languages They Encode*

Cambridge University Press, 530 pp. Published digitally 20 March 2026, hardback 2 April 2026.

## What it claims

'Syllabotactic analysis': a method for testing whether two undeciphered scripts encode the same
language from system-internal sign-position (syllable-combination) constraints, applied across
the book's chapters to Linear A, Cretan Hieroglyphic, Cypro-Minoan and Eteocypriot, with Linear
B and the Cypriot Syllabary as decipherable controls. Chapter 11 concludes that Cypro-Minoan and
Linear A "clearly encode different languages," while Cypro-Minoan and Eteocypriot encode the
same language. That Linear A is not Greek is a background premise of the whole book rather than
a result argued for.

## Our assessment

Checked 2026-09-13: could not reach the primary text; the flag stays **P**, now based on the
publisher's own chapter abstracts rather than a review, since none exists yet. Corrected the
title to include its subtitle, confirmed via Crossref. Confirmed the page count (530 pp.) and
the different-languages conclusion for Cypro-Minoan versus Linear A directly against the
Cambridge Core abstract for chapter 11 ("Syllabotactic Analysis of Eteocypriot and Linear A
against Cypro-Minoan"), which states in these terms that "Cypro-Minoan and Linear A clearly
encode different languages."

The citation "Donnelly (BMCR 2026.01.17) calls syllabotactic analysis 'contentious within
linguistics'" was wrong and is removed. BMCR 2026.01.17 is Cassandra Donnelly's review of a
different book, Civitillo, Ferrara and Meißner's *Cretan Hieroglyphic* (Cambridge University
Press, 2024), published in January 2026, before Davis's book existed (it was not published until
March 2026), so it cannot be a review of this monograph. That review's own wording is also
different from the quote on this page: it says syllabotactics "has all but been discarded within
the field of linguistics," a stronger claim than "contentious", and it is aimed at the syllabotactic
chapter in the *Cretan Hieroglyphic* volume, not at Davis's book. No BMCR review of *The
Undeciphered Aegean Scripts* itself has appeared as of this check.

The claim that the book's appendix of 66 sign-groups is the neutral word list that Di Mino's
*Ya Diktu* (`dimino2026` in this catalogue) supplies comparanda for is not confirmed from the
primary, 2026-09-13. No accessible source (publisher page, chapter abstracts, or reviews)
mentions such an appendix, and it could not be checked against the book itself.

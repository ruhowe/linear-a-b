# Ringe (1992), *On Calculating the Factor of Chance in Language Comparison*

Transactions of the American Philosophical Society 82(1).

## What it claims

A probabilistic method for how many lexical matches between two word lists chance alone
produces, so that resemblance between unrelated languages can be priced.

- DOI [10.2307/1006563](https://doi.org/10.2307/1006563)

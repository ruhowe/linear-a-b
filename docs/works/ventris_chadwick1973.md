# Ventris and Chadwick (1973), *Documents in Mycenaean Greek*, second edition

Cambridge University Press; first edition 1956. Read 2026-09-12 from the PDF: Part I
chapter II §3 (Linear A, pp. 31–37 of the book), §5 (the spelling rules, pp. 42–48), and
chapter III §§5–8 (the language: phonology, morphology, syntax, vocabulary, pp. 76–90).
The personal-names chapter (pp. 92–105) is extracted and used by the answer-key build.

## What it says that this project uses

**Morphology (pp. 83–89), the source of the reference list.** Nouns: feminine ā-stems
sing. -a throughout, plur. nom./acc. -a, gen. -a-o, dat. -a-i, instr. -a-pi; masculine
ā-stems the same with gen. sing. -a-o. o-stems sing. -o, gen. -o-jo, dat. -o; dual -o;
plur. -o, gen. -o, dat. -o-i, instr. -o (Knossos -o-pi); neuters -o sing., -a plur.
Consonant stems: nom. sing. zero, acc. -a, gen. -o, dat. -e or -i; dual -e; plur. -e,
-a, -o, dat. -si, instr. -pi (po-me, po-me-ne, po-me-no; wa-na-ka, wa-na-ka-te). s-stems
-e, gen. -e-o, dat. -e-i, dual -e-e, neut. plur. -e-a / -e-a2, dat. -e-si, instr. -e-pi.
u-stems -u, plur. -u-we, dat. -u-we, instr. -u-pi. eu-stems -e-u, -e-wo, -e-we (-e-wi),
plur. -e-we, -e-u-si, -e-u-pi. Adjectives in -went-: masc. -we, gen. -we-to, fem. -we-sa.
Participles: -o / -o-te, -me-no / -me-na, perf. -u-ja / -wo-a. Verbs: 3 sing. -e, 3 plur.
-o-si (thematic), -si / -si (athematic), infin. -e-e, future -se / -so-si, middle -to,
aorist -sa-to. Word formation: feminines in -e-ja from masculines in -e-u (i-je-re-u /
i-je-re-ja); adjectives -e-wi-jo / -e-wi-ja; material adjectives -e-jo / -e-o / -i-jo;
-te-ro. Spelling variants -a / -a2, -ja / -a2, ra / ra2, and the rest of §13.

**Spelling rules (pp. 42–48).** §2 the second element of ai, ei, oi is omitted (dat.
sing., nom. plur.); §4 p/ph, k/g/kh, t/th share a series, d has its own; §5 l and r share
a series; §7 the labiovelar series q; §8 final -l, -m, -n, -r, -s at the end of a
syllable are omitted (ka-ko = khalkos, pa-te = pantes); §9 initial s- before a consonant is
generally omitted (ta-to-mo = stathmos, pe-ma = sperma); §10 a plosive before another
consonant is written with the vowel of the following syllable (ko-no-so, ku-ru-so), and
-rw- usually drops the r; §14 middle or final signs of long formula words are sometimes
omitted; §15 enclitics -qe and -de are joined to the preceding word.

**On Linear A (pp. 31–37).** "The possible similarities which have been shown with
'Hieroglyphs' or with Linear B signs should not be taken to argue an identical
sound-value; such an identity could only be proved by a cogent decipherment, which in
view of our complete ignorance of the 'Minoan' language is probably impossible." And
Bennett 1953, quoted at p. 37: "where the same sign is used in both Linear A and B there
is no guarantee that the same value is assigned to it." One possible grammatical
variation in Linear A is noted, a pair on two libation vessels that under Linear B
values would read no-pi-na-ma-[ and no-pi-na-mi-na; ku-ro and ki-ro as the total and
subtotal words at Hagia Triada. Of the Linear A words written entirely in signs
paralleled in Linear B, "only one or two" recur on a Linear B inscription.

## What it verifies for this project

- The reference list of Mycenaean alternations (kober-method.md) was written from memory
  of this chapter and checked against it only now. What the chapter has and the list
  lacked, beyond the ethnic derivation added in 0.6: the consonant-stem case endings
  (-ne/-no, -te/-ta, zero/-Ce), the s-stem set (-e-a, -e-si, -e-pi, -e-e), the u-stems,
  the eu-stem plural (-e-u-si, -e-u-pi), the -went- adjectives, the -o/-o-te participle,
  the infinitive -e-e, the feminine -e-u/-e-ja, the material-adjective triplet, and the
  spelling variants in -a2 and ra2. Rebuilt as reference list v3, Kober 0.8, from this
  page and the chapter directly.
- A-005, the expansion rules of the etymological instrument: the coda rule (omitted l, r,
  m, n, s before a consonant; omitted final s, n, r) matches §8. §9, initial s- before a
  consonant omitted, is not in the expansion; stated limit, recorded on A-005.
- A-001: the primary source of the decipherment states the value-transfer caution in
  the same words the repository uses.

## Our assessment

The reference. Its morphology chapter is the answer key any Linear B positive control
should be scored against, and the project's list was three revisions short of it. Its
Linear A pages are a model of restraint.

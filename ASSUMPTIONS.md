# Assumptions

Every assumption the project's results rest on, numbered, with where it enters, what it
would change if wrong, and whether it has been tested. This is the register the first
question in `docs/ai_context/result-discipline.md` asks for: what is the instrument
taking as given?

Rules. An assumption is entered here the moment it is made, not when it is questioned.
None is deleted; a retired or falsified one is marked. A FINDINGS entry that depends on
an assumption cites it by number. A new lexicon, map or protocol version is not complete
until its assumptions are here.

Status values: **untested**, **tested (F-nnn)**, **falsified (F-nnn)**, **stated limit**.
A stated limit is a known simplification that is accepted and disclosed rather than
tested.

| A-063 | `--subsample-docs K --subsample-seed S` (`kober_run.py`) and the floor sweep's tablet model (`kober_floor_sweep.py`) draw their document subset as the first `K` ids of one seeded full permutation of the sorted document-id list (`words.py::subsample_document_ids`: sort, then `random.Random(S).shuffle`, then slice), not as an independent `random.Random(S).sample(pool, K)` draw per `K` (the scheme `subsample_word_types` uses for word types, A-057).

# di Mino (2026), *Ya Diktu: Grammar of the Minoan Peak Sanctuary Libation Formula*

Zenodo (preprint, not peer reviewed).

## What it claims

Minoan is 'Tinitic', an independent Central Semitic branch with Eteocretan as its descendant. 42 sign readings, a 508-entry lexicon, 443 translations. Claims prefix conjugation, tG/N stems, mimation, energic nun.

## Our assessment

Verification **Y**. We fetched and read the primary source.

CC BY 4.0, v5 dated 27 Aug 2026. Reclassified from 'fringe' after reading it directly: it cites the specialist literature accurately, quotes the backward-projection objection rather than ignoring it, reconciles a 1,780-inscription corpus, and was discussed with Gary Rendsburg (Rutgers Semitist). Citation audit passed — his inscription references are not fabricated. Two measured weaknesses: only 67% of his central inscription rests on demonstrably-shared values, and he re-divides word-groups the edition does not (2 of 7 lexemes on KN Zc 7 exist only under his re-segmentation). No code or data released, so it cannot be re-run by anyone including him.

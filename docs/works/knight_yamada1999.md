# Knight, Yamada (1999), *A Computational Approach to Deciphering Unknown Scripts*

ACL Workshop on Unsupervised Learning in NLP.

## What it claims

EM phonetic decipherment of Japanese kana with a learning curve: "100 sentences yields 97.5%
accuracy; 50 sentences yields 96.2% accuracy; 20 sentences yields 82.2% accuracy; five
sentences yields 48.5% accuracy".

## Where to find it

- [aclanthology.org/W99-0906](https://aclanthology.org/W99-0906/)
- Access: **open**

## Our assessment

Verification **Y**. We fetched and read the primary source.

An accuracy-against-corpus-size curve on a syllabary, the Shown-elsewhere precedent for
measuring a corpus floor. The language and sound link are known in advance, so it is not a
structural test.

Graded in the prior-art ledger of [claims.md](../ai_context/claims.md).

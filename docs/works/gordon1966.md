# Gordon (1966), *Evidence for the Minoan Language*

Ventnor Publishers.

## What it claims

Minoan is Semitic, argued from matches of Linear A words, read with Linear B values, to
Semitic vocabulary.

## Where to find it

- OCLC 748577 (predates ISBN)
- Access: **print-only**

## Our assessment

Verification **S**. We have only seen a search snippet. Treat every number in this record as
unconfirmed.

By a search snippet, reviewers objected that the matches are drawn across several Semitic
languages rather than one. That is an argument about permissive matching, not a measurement.

Graded in the prior-art ledger of [claims.md](../ai_context/claims.md).

Checked 2026-09-13: no local copy in `private/` or `~/.cache/linear-a-b/reference/`, and no
open route found (print-only, not on archive.org, which holds a different Gordon title,
*Ugarit and Minoan Crete*, not this one). Confirmed title, author, publisher, place and
year, and confirmed OCLC 748577 directly against the Open Library edition record (44 pages
plus 12 plates, Ventnor Publishers, Ventnor, N.J., 1966): no correction needed. A separate
ISBN (978-0-911566-06-2) circulates on retail sites for what appears to be a later printing,
not the edition OCLC 748577 describes; not added here since it does not identify the 1966
printing. The Semitic-matching claim and the "several Semitic languages" review point stand
as before; nothing here was read from the primary. Flag stays S.

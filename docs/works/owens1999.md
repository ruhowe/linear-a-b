# Owens (1999), *The Structure of the Minoan Language*, and *Kritika Daidalika* (ed., 1997)

Owens, "The Structure of the Minoan Language," *Journal of Indo-European Studies* 27
(1999), pp. 15-56. Related: Gareth Alun Owens (ed.), *'Kritika Daidalika': Evidence for
the Minoan Language. Selected Essays in Memory of James Hooker on the Archaeology,
Epigraphy and Philology of Minoan and Mycenaean Crete* (Hakkert, Amsterdam and Las
Palmas de Gran Canaria, 1997), a 20-essay Festschrift Owens edited, containing his own
essay arguing for Minoan readings under transferred Linear B and Cypriot syllabary
values.

## What it claims

By a search snippet and by two later descriptions of the Festschrift essay (not the
primary): reads Linear A with transferred Linear B and Cypriot syllabary values and
argues Minoan is Indo-European, related to Greek, Sanskrit and Armenian. Not confirmed
from the primary, 2026-09-13.

Checked 2026-09-13: this record previously carried the title "Kritika Daidalika and
related work on Linear A" with year 1999 and no identifiers. That title, dated 1999, does
not exist: *'Kritika Daidalika': Evidence for the Minoan Language* is a Festschrift for
James Hooker, edited (not authored) by Gareth Alun Owens, published by A.M. Hakkert in
1997, confirmed against WorldCat (OCLC 38166397), HathiTrust and Google Books, all giving
1997. Old to new: title "Kritika Daidalika and related work on Linear A" (1999, no
identifier) corrected to two named works, *The Structure of the Minoan Language* (Owens,
*JIES* 27, 1999, pp. 15-56, a genuine 1999 item) and *Kritika Daidalika* (ed. Owens,
Hakkert, 1997, ISBN 90-256-1096-X, OCLC 38166397); "authors: [Owens]" for the Festschrift
corrected to editor, since it collects 20 essays by various contributors and Owens's own
argument is one essay within it. No local copy of either work and no open route were
found, so neither claim was confirmed from the primary; both are marked as such. Flag
stays S.

## What it claims

Language-ID across 380 candidates applied to the Voynich manuscript; Hebrew ranked top.

## Our assessment

Verification **Y**. We fetched and read the primary source.

THE anti-pattern, and honest about it: authors self-hedge that results may be 'artifacts of the combinatorial power of anagramming'. Stacked unverifiable assumptions — substitution plus anagram plus abjad.

Added 2026-09-11 (prior-art search): the paper's language identification on known texts reaches 97% over 380 languages with character n-gram models on UDHR texts, about 1,800 words per language. Matching can choose a language given enough text and that method, which bounds the repo's C-1 to dictionary matching at Aegean scale.

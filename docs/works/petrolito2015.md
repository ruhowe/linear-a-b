# Petrolito, Petrolito, Perono Cacciafoco, Winterstein (2015), *Minoan linguistic resources: the Linear A Digital Corpus*

LaTeCH@ACL (9th SIGHUM Workshop on Language Technology for Cultural Heritage, Social
Sciences, and Humanities, ACL 2015, Beijing), pp. 95–104. Read in full from the ACL
Anthology PDF. The byline, per the ACL Anthology record, is Tommaso Petrolito, Ruggero Petrolito,
Francesco Perono Cacciafoco and Grégoire Winterstein.

## What it claims

A marked-up digital corpus of Linear A: a collection of TEI-EpiDoc XML documents built
from John G. Younger's phonetic transcriptions and the GORILA catalogue, converted to
Unicode 7.0's Linear A character block (released June 2014), with a new font
(John_Younger.ttf) released to make the Unicode range readable on unsupported systems.
The corpus and font were to be published at `http://ling.ied.edu.HK/~gregoire/lineara`.

## Our assessment

Verification **Y**. We fetched and read the primary source in full.

Braović 2024 reports being unable to find it online. A cautionary case: a published resource that evaporated. What evaporated is the corpus and its intended URL; the paper itself has stayed freely available on the ACL Anthology.

Checked 2026-09-13: read the full ACL Anthology PDF (previously recorded as "not
findable online", per the hint that prompted this check). Confirmed the claim ("a
marked-up digital corpus of Linear A") and expanded it with the paper's own description
of its format (TEI-EpiDoc XML, Unicode 7.0, a new font). Confirmed the byline order against the ACL Anthology record (the first note of this
check reversed the last two authors in error; the session review restored the record's
order). Corrected access from offline to
open: the paper itself is on the ACL Anthology and always has been; it is the corpus
deliverable's own promised URL that is gone, which is what Braović 2024 and this page's
prior note actually refer to. Upgraded verification from P to Y accordingly.

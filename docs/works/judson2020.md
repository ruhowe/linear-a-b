# Judson (2020), *The Undeciphered Signs of Linear B: Interpretation and Scribal Practices*

Cambridge University Press.

## What it claims

14 of 87 syllabograms lack agreed values. In the PhD thesis the book developed from, the
14 are *18, *19, *22, *34, *47, *49, *56, *63, *64, *65, *79, *82, *83 and *86; the thesis
calls *65 = ju "very probably" right and *56 = pa₃ "probably" right, and says of *64 = swi
that the equation it rests on is "somewhat less secure than is often assumed" and the value
"remains open." This page earlier gave the book's wording as "extremely probable" (*65),
"most likely" (*56) and "plausible" (*64), from reviews of the book; the book itself has
not been read, so which wording it prints is not confirmed. The claim that *18 and *19 have Cretan Hieroglyphic parallels is not confirmed
from the primary; the underlying thesis discusses possible Linear A correspondences for
these signs, not Cretan Hieroglyphic ones, and states outright that *18 "has no generally
accepted LA correspondence." The claim that only ~3 signs are Linear B inventions is not
confirmed from the primary, 2026-09-13: the thesis describes many more candidate LB-only
inventions (a2, a3, the dwe/dwo/twe/two/pte set, ra3, ro2 and others).

## Our assessment

Verification **P**. We have only seen this restated elsewhere, in a review, index record or later paper. Re-verify before citing it in print.

Explicitly claims no decipherments. In the thesis the 14 are *18, *19, *22, *34, *47, *49, *56, *63, *64, *65, *79, *82, *83 and *86; *89 is excluded by name ("probably a pseudo-sign"), and no sign *35 appears. The book's list is expected to match and has not been checked against the book. These signs appear in our restoration target vocabulary as *NNN labels.

Checked 2026-09-13: read Judson's PhD thesis in full (the dissertation this monograph is
based on, openly deposited at the Cambridge Apollo repository), not the CUP monograph
itself, which stayed paywalled at every route we tried. Confirmed "14 of 87 syllabograms
lack agreed values" and the "no decipherments" framing directly. Corrected the exact list
of the 14 signs, which the thesis gives explicitly and which excludes *89 (called out by
name as excluded, being "probably a pseudo-sign") and does not include any sign called
*35; the page previously named "*34/*35, *47, *89" as examples, which is wrong on two of
three. Recorded the thesis's wording on *65, *56 and *64 beside the book-review wording the page
had, without asserting which the book prints (session review, 2026-09-13). Could not confirm
"*18, *19 have Cretan Hieroglyphic parallels" or "only ~3 signs are Linear B inventions"
from the thesis; the first appears to conflate Cretan Hieroglyphic with Linear A, and the
second is contradicted by the thesis's own catalogue of candidate LB-only extra signs.
Kept verification at P rather than upgrading to Y, since the document read is the
underlying thesis rather than the published monograph itself, and the two may differ in
places; a reader citing this page in print should still get the book.

# S-002 · The etymological method's own floor

**Question.** F-013 found Greek's lead over the best wrong real language on the full
Linear B corpus to be about one sample standard error. The Kober method's floor was
measured by subsampling (F-022). What is the etymological method's floor: at what Linear
B size does protocol 1.0 separate Greek from Finnish and Sumerian by, say, two standard
errors across twenty subsamples? Expectation, written now: never, within the corpus. If
so, the method's floor lies above the whole of Linear B, which is a sharper statement
than "cannot choose a language".

**Why a spike.** The etymological line is closed; this reopens no question about Linear
A, it characterises a closed instrument. Cheap: protocol 1.0 on tablet-model subsamples
at 988, 1,875, 2,750 and 3,768 word types, Greek archaic, Finnish and Sumerian, Greek
map, twenty subsamples each.

**Interesting.** A separation appears at some size: then the method has a floor and it
can be quoted. **Nothing.** No separation at any size, as expected: quoted as the
instrument's floor exceeding the corpus.

**Must not be read as.** Evidence about any language on Linear A.

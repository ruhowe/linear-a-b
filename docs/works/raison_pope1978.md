# Raison, Pope (1978), *Linear A: changing perspectives*

In Duhoux (ed.), Études minoennes I, Louvain (Peeters), pp. 5-64. Same volume as
[duhoux1978](duhoux1978.md), which follows this chapter at pp. 65-129. Raison and Pope
also contributed a second, separate chapter to the same volume, "Le vocabulaire du
linéaire A en translitération" (pp. 131-190); that chapter is not this page's subject.

## What it claims

By eye: reviews the perspective the Linear B decipherment opened on Linear A,
arguing the two syllabaries' sign inventories overlap more than earlier writers
allowed, and reading Linear A sign-groups under transferred Linear B values.
Offers identical or analogous readings for place names (*pa-i-to*, *ku-do-ni*,
*i-da-jo*, *a-su-ja*) and personal names (*da-i-pi-ta*, *di-de-ru*, *pa-ja-re*,
*qa-qa-ru*, *qa-ra-wa*). A search snippet attributed to this chapter (via a
secondary source, unconfirmed against the primary) claims a further ratio
breakdown of Packard's alternation counts by findspot, Knossos versus the
mainland; a book review of the volume (Persée, 1983) describes only the
place/personal-name readings above and does not mention a ratio breakdown, so
that claim is not carried into this page.

## Our assessment

Verification **S**. Seen only through a 1983 Persée book review of the volume
and web search snippets; the primary chapter was not read. Treat every detail
here as unconfirmed until the primary is read.

By-eye readings of Linear A place names under transferred Linear B values, the
same move Packard made systematically the same year (1974) and Ventris and
Chadwick made cautiously in 1973 (only "one or two" full-word matches). No
count against a chance model, and no wrong-lexicon control — the readings are
asserted, not measured. Graded in the prior-art ledger of
[claims.md](../ai_context/claims.md) for C-8a.

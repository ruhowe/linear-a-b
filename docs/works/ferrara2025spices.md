# Ferrara, Salgarella, Bellinato (2025), *On Aegean spices: decipherment prospects on Linear A and B spice-related signs*

Kadmos 64(1-2): 29–44. Part of the ERC project INSCRIBE (Invention of Scripts and their
Beginnings), awarded to S. Ferrara.

## What it claims

Two logograms read without recourse to Linear B sound values: *157, a composite sign shared by
Linear B and Linear A (A646) built around an S-shaped element, read as a tuberous plant root
(compared to calamus), on the proposed grounds that it depicts a root and echoes the Mycenaean
Greek word *wi-ri-za*, "root". The monogram KA+PO, distinguished from the syllabically spelled
ka-po ("fruit"), is read as "dry stalk" or "dry branch", following a 1972 proposal by Anna
Sacconi. Both readings come from contextual analysis of tablets recording distributions of raw
materials to perfume-makers, compared with graphic parallels between Linear B and Linear A,
without touching sound values.

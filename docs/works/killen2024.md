# Killen (2024), *The New Documents in Mycenaean Greek*

Cambridge University Press, 2 vols. John Killen (ed.), ISBN 9781009286091, 1199 pp.

## What it claims

Standard reference succeeding Ventris & Chadwick 1973, though not a straightforward
replacement: the BMCR review notes the only part carried over unchanged from the 1973
edition is Ventris's own chapter on "Discovery and decipherment," the rest being a new,
multi-authored work.

## Where to find it

- ISBN 9781009286091
- Access: **print-only**

## Our assessment

Verification **P**. We read the BMCR review directly (not a search snippet); the primary two volumes were not read. Re-verify before citing it in print.

Metrology chapters flagged as hard even for specialists (BMCR 2024.12.20): "the essays
discussing the absolute values of the units for weight and volume (Chapter IV.6-7) are
extremely lengthy technical discussions, and not easy to follow even for a Linear B
specialist."

Checked 2026-09-13: confirmed editor (John Killen), publisher, 2-volume structure, ISBN
9781009286091 and page count (1199) against BMCR 2024.12.20 and Cambridge University
Press's own catalogue pages, and added the ISBN, which was missing. Read the BMCR review
in full rather than a snippet of it, confirming the metrology-chapters claim with the
quotation above and adding the nuance, now in "What it claims," that the volume replaces
Ventris and Chadwick's role rather than reprinting their text. The two volumes themselves
remain unread. Flag stays P.

# Aurora (2015), *DĀMOS (Database of Mycenaean at Oslo): Annotating a Fragmentarily Attested Language*

Procedia SBS 198, 21–31. The title on this page previously omitted the subtitle, and the
page range was missing; both are corrected from Crossref.

## What it claims

Relational database: item / phys_line / phys_word / phys_syllables, epigraphic status as
attributes. This level of schema detail is not confirmed from the primary, 2026-09-13; the
routes we reached (Crossref, a 2024 follow-up paper by the same author, secondary
summaries) confirm a relational database with rich epigraphic, linguistic and content
annotation, but not these specific table or field names.

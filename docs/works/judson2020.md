# Judson (2020), *The Undeciphered Signs of Linear B: Interpretation and Scribal Practices*

Cambridge University Press.

## What it claims

14 of 87 syllabograms lack agreed values. *65 = ju 'extremely probable'; *56 = pa₃ most likely; *64 = swi plausible. *18, *19 have Cretan Hieroglyphic parallels; only ~3 signs are Linear B inventions.

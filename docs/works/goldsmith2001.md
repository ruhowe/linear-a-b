# Goldsmith (2001), *Unsupervised Learning of the Morphology of a Natural Language*

Computational Linguistics 27(2):153-198.

## What it claims

An MDL algorithm (Linguistica) that induces stems, suffixes and paradigms without supervision.

- DOI [10.1162/089120101750300490](https://doi.org/10.1162/089120101750300490)

## Our assessment

Verification **Y**. We fetched and read the primary source.

The computational lineage of the Kober method as automated here. Alphabetic, data-rich
languages, never Linear B.

Graded in the prior-art ledger of [claims.md](../ai_context/claims.md).

Checked 2026-09-13: read the full text from the ACL Anthology
(https://aclanthology.org/J01-2001.pdf), open access. Title, sole author, journal, volume,
issue and pages all confirmed against Crossref and the PDF's own header. The claim that
the paper describes an MDL algorithm named Linguistica that induces stems, suffixes and
paradigms without supervision is confirmed: the software is named "Linguistica" throughout,
morphological patterns are termed "signatures," and section 9 is titled "Paradigms."
Nothing on the page needed correction.
